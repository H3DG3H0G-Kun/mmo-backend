## A. The Light of Kartli — წმინდა ნინო & სვეტიცხოველი
`content/the-light-of-kartli.json` · HISTORY · era **NINO (5)** · resonance `ERA=NINO`+`PLACE=mtskheta` · unlocks 6.
- **Source:** the Life of St Nino (Kartlis Tskhovreba): the Cappadocian maiden, the **grapevine
  cross** bound with her hair, the healing of Queen **Nana**, King **Mirian's** darkness on the
  hunt that lifted when he called on Nino's God (~337), and the raising of **Svetitskhoveli** — the
  cedar grown over the buried **Robe of Christ** (Elioz, Sidonia), the pillar that would not rise
  until Nino's night of prayer, then streamed holy myron and healed: *„სვეტი ცხოველი", the
  life-giving pillar.*
- **Interactions:** Witness Nino's coming → Aid the raising of the cross → Witness the king's
  conversion → **Restore** the living pillar (the core ritual) → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the Aspect of the Old Dark — the *forgetting* that would keep the pillar from
  rising; **never** a mockery of the pagan cult and **never** peril to Nino herself (COSMOLOGY
  §6.2–6.3). Highest reverence; sacred chant only here (SOUND §6).

## B. The Wolf-Head's City — ვახტანგ გორგასალი
`content/the-wolf-heads-city.json` · HISTORY · era **VAKHTANG (6)** · resonance `ERA=VAKHTANG`+`PLACE=tbilisi_springs` · unlocks 7.
- **Source:** Juansher's life of **Vakhtang Gorgasali** (5th c.), the *wolf-head* king (his helmet
  terrified the Persians). The founding legend of **Tbilisi**: hunting, his falcon downed a
  pheasant into a **warm spring** — *tbili*, "warm" — and there he founded the city and moved the
  capital from Mtskheta. The great defender of Kartli against the Persian tide.
- **Interactions:** Witness the hunt and the warm springs → Aid the founding (lay the first stone)
  → Choose (build by the springs / fortify Kala) rejoin → **Ward** the young city → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the Aspect of the Foreign Tide (Persian/Byzantine pressure that would un-found the
  city) — not a people, the *erasing pressure.*

## C. Abo of Tiflis — აბო თბილელი
`content/abo-of-tiflis.json` · HISTORY/WORD · era **ABO (7)** · resonance `ERA=ABO`+`PLACE=metekhi_cliff` · unlocks 8.
- **Source:** Ioane Sabanisdze's *Martyrdom of Abo* (8th c.). **Abo**, an Arab perfumer who came to
  Kartli, embraced Christianity, and was martyred in Tbilisi (786) under Arab rule; his body burned
  and ashes cast from the **Metekhi cliff** into the Mtkvari; the **patron saint of Tbilisi**.
- **Interactions:** Witness Abo's coming and conviction → Witness his trial → **Ward** the *memory*
  of his witness (not his life — he is martyred, per Host-&-Guest's pattern) → Restore the city's
  remembrance → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the Long Night / the Unremembering as the crescent-tide *political erasure* of
  Kartli's name. **Sensitivity (binding):** Abo was a convert *from Islam*; Islam and the Arab
  people are **not** the villain — the antagonist is the Unremembering, the erasure of a city's
  faith and name. Depict with the dignity COSMOLOGY §6.4 demands.

## D. Didgori — დავით აღმაშენებელი
`content/didgori.json` · HISTORY · era **AGHMASHENEBELI (9)** · resonance `ERA=AGHMASHENEBELI`+`PLACE=didgori` · unlocks 3.
- **Source:** **David IV the Builder** (დავით აღმაშენებელი) and the **Battle of Didgori, 12 Aug
  1121** — *„ძლევაჲ საკვირველი", the wondrous victory* over the Seljuk coalition; the recovery of
  **Tbilisi (1122)**; **Gelati**; his humility (his *Hymns of Repentance*, his grave laid at the
  gate to be trodden).
- **Interactions:** Witness the eve and the king's prayer → Aid the muster → **Ward** the line at
  Didgori (the great defensive stand) → Restore Tbilisi to the crown → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the Aspect of the Invading Tide — the erasing force, never the historical foe as a
  caricature.

  Choose (follow Avtandil / stay with Tariel's grief) rejoin → **Ward** against a **Kaji** (the

## G. The Self-Sacrificer — დემეტრე თავდადებული
`content/the-self-sacrificer.json` · HISTORY · era **SUNDERING (10)** · resonance `ERA=SUNDERING`+`PLACE=ordu` · unlocks 11.
- **Source:** **Demetre II "the Self-Sacrificer"** (თავდადებული), king under the Mongol Ilkhanate;
  summoned amid a plot, he **chose to go and be executed (1289)** rather than let Georgia suffer
  reprisal — and is venerated as a saint. The Watcher's own mirror: a soul that gave itself for its
  people's memory.
- **Interactions:** Witness the summons and the impossible choice → Choose (witness his counsel /
  the people he shields) rejoin → Witness his going → **Ward** the *meaning* of the sacrifice
  against forgetting → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the Unremembering that would erase his sacrifice into "just another death." The
  **Mongols are a historical force / the Aspect**, never a vilified living people (COSMOLOGY §6).

## H. The Great Mouravi — გიორგი სააკაძე
`content/the-great-mouravi.json` · HISTORY · era **SAAKADZE (11)** · resonance `ERA=SAAKADZE`+`PLACE=marabda` · unlocks 12.
- **Source:** **Giorgi Saakadze**, the **Didi Mouravi** (Great Mouravi), 17th c.; the tragic patriot
  who strove to unite a fractured Georgia; **Martqopi** and **Marabda (1625)** against Shah Abbas's
  Persia. A genuinely **contested** figure — we honor the **national memory** of him as a patriot
  *while naming the complexity honestly* (COSMOLOGY §6.6).
- **Interactions:** Witness the divided land → Aid the muster of a fragile unity → **Ward** at
  Marabda → Choose (the cost of unity / the grief of division) rejoin → terminal Witness (the
  lesson: division is the Unremembering's doorway).
- **Distortion:** the **Aspect of Division (განხეთქილება)** — the feud *among Georgians* that the
  Unremembering exploits. Ties to GAME_DESIGN §7's "fragmentation → unity" theme and the memory-war.

## I. Krtsanisi — კრწანისი & the Three Hundred Aragvians
`content/krtsanisi.json` · HISTORY · era **EREKLE (12)** · resonance `ERA=EREKLE`+`PLACE=krtsanisi` · unlocks 13.
- **Source:** **Erekle II** (Heraclius II) and the **Battle of Krtsanisi (Sept 1795)**, when Agha
  Mohammad Khan's vast army faced a few thousand Georgians and sacked Tbilisi; the **Three Hundred
  Aragvians (სამასი არაგველი)** who died covering the king's retreat. The end of the old kingdom;
  immense pathos.
- **Interactions:** Witness the aged king and the impossible odds → Aid the last muster → **Ward**
  the retreat with the Three Hundred (the great last stand) → Restore the *memory* of their sacrifice
  → terminal Witness (the burning city; the kingdom passes, the memory does not).
- **Distortion:** the Aspect of the Burning — the erasing tide that sacked Tbilisi; **not** the
  Persian people. Honor the dead; restraint, not spectacle.

## J. Aluda Ketelauri — ალუდა ქეთელაური
`content/aluda-ketelauri.json` · WORD · era **MODERN_WORD (13)** · resonance `PLACE=mountain`+`PRIOR_TALE=TALE_HOST_AND_GUEST` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** **Vazha-Pshavela's ალუდა ქეთელაური (1888).** The Khevsur **Aluda** kills the Kist
  raider **Mutsali** in fair combat but, moved by his enemy's bravery, **refuses to cut off his
  hand** as a trophy — then insists on **sacrificing an ox for the soul of the slain Muslim**, which
  the **khevisberi** and the community condemn as sacrilege; Aluda is **exiled** with his family. The
  companion humanist masterpiece to Host & Guest — *the enemy is also a human being.*
- **Resonance note:** gated by **PRIOR_TALE = Host & Guest** — having restored one Vazha highland
  tragedy opens its twin. (Uses the `PRIOR_TALE` trigger to chain the two.)
- **Interactions:** Witness the combat and Mutsali's bravery → **Choose** (honor the enemy / take
  the trophy as custom demands) — but BOTH true branches lead Aluda to honor him (the choice is
  *how you witness his conscience*, never a rewrite) → Witness the ox-offering and the temi's wrath
  → **Ward** the meaning against the Aspect of Tribal Pride → terminal Witness (the exile; the
  lonely rightness).
- **Distortion:** the **Aspect of Tribal Pride / the temi's blindness** — never the Khevsur people,
  never the faith. The same humanist law as Host & Guest (COSMOLOGY §6.1–6.2).
