# Tales Catalog — ten more (literature, folk, faith, and the first ECHOes)

> Compact design notes for ten further tales. They (1) **debut the ECHO tier** — "what-if/corrupted
> memory," where harder fights and variation are allowed *because the fiction marks them not-real*
> (GAME_DESIGN §4); (2) widen the **tonal range** (a comic trickster, a folk romance, a lullaby-
> song); and (3) deepen the **Word** and **faith** strands. Same rules as the other catalogs: obey
> [../COSMOLOGY.md](../COSMOLOGY.md) §6; the boss is never the beloved figure; verify exact ქართული
> before final canon.

---

## Word / literature (the 19th-century Reborn Word, + a highland epic)

### P. The Hermit — განდეგილი  (Ilia Chavchavadze)
`content/the-hermit.json` · WORD · era **MODERN_WORD (13)** · `PLACE=mountain_cave`+`STATE=STORM` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** Ilia Chavchavadze's *განდეგილი* (1883). A monk who has renounced the world for decades
  in a cave high on the mountain (the Betlemi cave near Mqinvartsveri/Kazbek); a young shepherdess,
  driven by a storm, seeks shelter; his lifelong vow is shaken, and he dies in spiritual crisis.
- **Treatment:** **utmost reverence.** Ilia is venerated as **წმინდა ილია მართალი** and called the
  Father of the Nation. The tale takes **no verdict** on faith vs. life — it *witnesses* a soul's
  tragedy. The Distortion is the **Aspect of Despair** that would have his whole devotion remembered
  as a meaningless waste; the Ward defends the *dignity of his struggle*, not a tidy answer. The
  shepherdess is not vilified — she is human, seeking shelter. Witness-heavy; near-silent (ART §7).

### Q. Suliko — სულიკო  (Akaki Tsereteli)
`content/suliko.json` · WORD · era **MODERN_WORD (13)** · `PLACE=grave`+`STATE=DUSK` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** Akaki Tsereteli's *სულიკო* (1895), the most beloved Georgian song. The speaker
  searches for the grave of the lost beloved Suliko and asks the **rose, the nightingale, the star**
  — finding her spirit alive in all three. *Love outlasting the grave is memory itself.*
- **Treatment:** the gentlest tale in the canon, a **CHOOSE** across the three (rose / nightingale /
  star — all true), resolving in a **RESTORE**: she lives wherever she is remembered. Ties directly
  to [../SOUND_DIRECTION.md](../SOUND_DIRECTION.md) — this *is* a polyphonic song; score it as one.

### R. Bakhtrioni — ბახტრიონი  (Vazha-Pshavela)
`content/bakhtrioni.json` · WORD · era **SAAKADZE (11)** · `ERA=SAAKADZE`+`PLACE=bakhtrioni` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** Vazha's epic of the **1659 Kakheti–highland uprising** and the storming of **Bakhtrioni**
  fortress, where Tush, Pshav, and Khevsur tribes unite (the heroine **Sanata**) to throw off the
  Qizilbash yoke. The unity-against-erasure theme in its most rousing key.
- **Interactions:** Witness the oppressed highlands → **Aid** the tribes' union → **Ward** Bakhtrioni
  → **Restore** the freed highlands → terminal Witness. Distortion: the Aspect of the Yoke.

## Folk / Myth (widening tone)

### S. Abesalom and Eteri — აბესალომ და ეთერი
`content/abesalom-and-eteri.json` · MYTH · home era **PARNAVAZ (1)** · `PLACE=meadow`+`STATE=DAWN` · unlocks null. **Imports now.**
- **Source:** the folk legend (basis of Paliashvili's opera *აბესალომ და ეთერი*). Prince **Abesalom**
  loves the shepherdess **Eteri**; the vizier **Murman** makes a pact with the devils to take her;
  the lovers are sundered and both die of grief.
- **Treatment:** a folk **tragedy of love**; the Distortion is **Murman's devil-bargain** (a Kaji-kin
  falsifier), never the lovers. We cannot save them (canon) — Ward + Restore keep the *memory* of a
  love the bargain could not erase.

### T. Natsarkekia — ნაცარქექია  (the trickster)
`content/natsarkekia.json` · MYTH · home era **PARNAVAZ (1)** · `PLACE=village`+`STATE=NIGHT` · unlocks null. **Imports now.**
- **Source:** the beloved folk trickster **ნაცარქექია** ("the ash-raker"), the lazy wit who outsmarts
  brute **devis** (the cheese-for-stone "squeeze water from rock," the strength contests).
- **Treatment:** **the canon's light, comic tale** — deliberately, for tonal range. Cleverness beats
  brute monsters; the devi is a fair monster-foe (like Kopala's dragon), warded with a wink, not
  grief. Proof the engine holds joy as readily as elegy.

## The first ECHOes (TIER = ECHO — "what-if," harder, marked not-real)

### U. Echo: The Fall That Was Not — ექო: დიდგორის ცრუ ხსოვნა
`content/echo-the-fall-that-was-not.json` · HISTORY · **TIER ECHO** · home era **AGHMASHENEBELI (9)** · `PRIOR_TALE=TALE_DIDGORI` · unlocks null.
- **Concept:** a **Kaji** conjures a corrupted memory in which Didgori was *lost* and Georgia erased.
  The Watcher enters the false field, refuses the lie, and through a **harder Ward** (ECHO permits it)
  restores the true ძლევაჲ საკვირველი. Opens only to one who has restored the true [Didgori](CATALOG_eras-5-13.md).
- **Why:** demonstrates how one true tale yields **infinitely replayable, harder content** without
  ever disrespecting the source — the false version is explicitly the enemy's lie, to be dispelled.

### V. Echo: The Grief That Swallowed — ექო: ჩაყლაპული გოდება
`content/echo-the-grief-that-swallowed.json` · WORD · **TIER ECHO** · home era **RUSTAVELI (4)** · `PRIOR_TALE=TALE_THE_TAKING_OF_KAJETI` · unlocks null. **Imports now.**
- **Concept:** the literary ECHO — a what-if where Avtandil never returned, Tariel despaired forever,
  and Nestan was left forgotten in Kajeti. A darker, **harder Kaji** at full strength. The Watcher
  refuses the false ending and restores the true one (the brotherhood, the freeing).
- **Why:** shows ECHO on the Word side — "what the silence imagined" — replayable without touching
  Rustaveli's real text.

## Faith / History (martyrs and the keepers of memory)

### W. Queen Ketevan the Martyr — ქეთევან დედოფალი
`content/queen-ketevan.json` · HISTORY · era **SAAKADZE (11)** · `ERA=SAAKADZE`+`PLACE=shiraz` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** **Queen Ketevan of Kakheti**, held by **Shah Abbas I** and tortured to death at Shiraz
  in **1624** for refusing to renounce her faith; a great venerated martyr-saint.
- **Treatment (binding):** the antagonist is the **coercion / the Unremembering**, *not* Islam or the
  Persian people (COSMOLOGY §6). We do not save her (canon); Ward defends the *meaning*, Restore keeps
  the memory the tyranny could not erase. Same humane frame as [Abo](CATALOG_eras-5-13.md).

### X. The Hundred Thousand — ასი ათასი მოწამე
`content/the-hundred-thousand.json` · HISTORY · era **SUNDERING (10)** · `ERA=SUNDERING`+`PLACE=metekhi_bridge` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** the **100,000 martyrs of Tbilisi (1227)**: when Jalal ad-Din sacked the city, the
  citizens were ordered to cross the Metekhi bridge by trampling the holy icons laid upon it, or die
  — and tens of thousands chose death. Commemorated to this day.
- **Treatment:** heaviest restraint (ART §7, SOUND §6); the erasing tide is the **Aspect**, never a
  people. Witness → Ward the meaning → Restore the memory → terminal Witness.

### Y. The Assyrian Fathers — ცამეტი ასურელი მამა
`content/the-assyrian-fathers.json` · HISTORY · era **VAKHTANG (6)** · `ERA=VAKHTANG`+`PLACE=davit_gareja` · unlocks null.
- **Source:** the **Thirteen Assyrian Fathers** (6th c.) who deepened Georgian Christianity and
  founded its monasticism — **David Garejeli** and the cave-monasteries of **Davit Gareja**,
  Shio-Mgvime, and others.
- **Treatment:** a gentle **founding/Restore** tale — and the most on-theme of all, because the
  monasteries the Fathers founded are literally **where memory was kept**: the scriptoria that copied
  and preserved the manuscripts across every Long Night. The keepers of memory, remembered.
