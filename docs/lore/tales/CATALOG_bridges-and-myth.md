## Bridges (History)

### K. The Watchfire of Armazi — არმაზის სახმილი  *(fills EARLY_KING → NINO)*
`content/the-watchfire-of-armazi.json` · HISTORY · era **EARLY_KING (2)** · `ERA=EARLY_KING`+`PLACE=armazi_mount` · **unlocks 5 (NINO)**.
- **Source/era:** post-Parnavaz pre-Christian Iberia — the Pharnavazid kings, the cult of **Armazi**
  on the holy mount above Mtskheta, the realm held between **Rome and Parthia**, and the **Armazi
  bilingual inscriptions** (Greek + Aramaic-"Armazic"), memory kept in stone.
- **Interactions:** Witness the rites of Armazi (honoured *with dignity* as the age's faith,
  COSMOLOGY §6.4 — never mocked, never "evil idol to smash") → Witness Iberia between the empires →
  **Aid** the inscribed memory (the stelae) → **Ward** identity → **Restore** the land kept whole →
  terminal Witness ("kept for the Light to come").
- **Distortion:** the Aspect of the Devouring Empires — the erasing pull to dissolve Iberia into a
  mere province. The bridge's theme: *the land kept itself intact so it could later receive the Light.*

### L. The Gathering — ბაგრატ III  *(fills the BAGRATI unification era 8)*
`content/the-gathering-bagrat.json` · HISTORY · era **BAGRATI (8)** · `ERA=BAGRATI`+`PLACE=kutaisi` · **unlocks 9 (AGHMASHENEBELI)**.
- **Source:** **Bagrat III** (r. ~1008), first king of a **unified Georgia** — Abkhazia, Kartli,
  Kakheti, and more drawn under one Bagrationi crown — and the great **Bagrati Cathedral** at
  Kutaisi (consecrated 1003) as the monument of that unity.
- **Interactions:** Witness the splintered crowns → **Aid** the gathering → **Ward** against the
  Aspect of Division → **Restore** (raise Bagrati Cathedral, the stone proof of one Georgia) →
  terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the **Aspect of Division (განხეთქილება)** — same family as Saakadze's; the
  fragmentation the Unremembering feeds on. Pairs the two ends of the unity/division theme.

## The Myth strand (trans-era; mostly no ERA gate)

### M. Dali of the Hunt — დალი
`content/dali-of-the-hunt.json` · MYTH · home era **PARNAVAZ (1)** · `PLACE=cliff`+`STATE=DAWN` (no ERA gate) · unlocks null. **Imports now.**
- **Source:** **დალი (Dali)**, the golden-haired goddess of the hunt and the high cliffs (Svan
  myth), who grants game to hunters who keep the sacred limit — and dooms those who break the taboo
  (over-killing, betrayal). The ancient **contract between the hunter and the wild.**
- **Interactions:** Witness Dali on the cliff → Witness the hunter's contract → **Choose** (witness
  the faithful hunter who keeps the limit / the doomed one who breaks it — both true faces of the
  myth, rejoin) → **Ward** the balance → **Restore** the contract → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the Aspect of Greed — the forgetting of the sacred limit (over-hunting, the
  broken taboo). An ecological myth: *remembering the limit is keeping the world.* Dali honoured as
  divine, never mocked (COSMOLOGY §6.4).

### N. Kopala and the Devis — კოპალა და დევები
`content/kopala-and-the-devis.json` · MYTH · home era **PARNAVAZ (1)** · `PLACE=gorge`+`STATE=NIGHT` (no ERA gate) · unlocks null. **Imports now.**
- **Source:** **კოპალა (Kopala)**, the Pshav-Khevsur hero-deity (a *khvtishvili*, child of Morige
  Ghmerti), who with his mace cleared the land of **devis** and the **გველეშაპი (gveleshapi,
  dragon)** so that humans could live in the gorges. Ties to the cosmology's highland pantheon.
- **Interactions:** Witness the devi-infested gorge → Witness Kopala's coming → **Aid** the clearing
  → **Ward** the **gveleshapi** (combat is *fully* appropriate here — the dragon/devi is a myth-
  monster, never a beloved figure: a clean Echo of primordial chaos) → **Restore** the spring the
  dragon swallowed → terminal Witness ("the land made fit for people").
- **Distortion:** the **გველეშაპი / devi** as a primordial chaos-Echo. The one tale in the slate
  where the boss is a literal monster of the myth — and that's exactly correct.

### O. Mindia the Snake-Eater — მინდია (გველის-მჭამელი)
`content/mindia-the-snake-eater.json` · WORD · home era **MODERN_WORD (13)** · `PLACE=forest`+`STATE=MIST` (no ERA gate) · unlocks null.
- **Source:** **Vazha-Pshavela's მინდია / „გველის-მჭამელი".** The captive Mindia eats the flesh of
  the snake/dragon hoping to die — and instead wakes to **the language of all living things**,
  living in healing harmony with nature; but the weight of family and community (war, wood, meat)
  forces him to break that harmony, the gift deserts him, and he despairs and dies. Vazha's great
  ecological-spiritual tragedy.
- **Interactions:** Witness the snake eaten → Witness the language of all things → Witness the
  weight of the world that forces the break → **Ward** the harmony against the Aspect of
  Disenchantment → **Restore** the *listening* (we cannot save Mindia — like Vazha's other tragic
  heroes — so we keep the *memory* that such harmony was once possible) → terminal Witness.
- **Distortion:** the **Aspect of Disenchantment** — the severing of humanity from nature's voice;
  the forgetting that we ever could listen. Thematic kin to Dali (the broken contract with the wild).
